      Why is there Blood on my Clocktower??

      The Ψ Press is an invite-only UChicago-based group that plays Blood on the Clocktower approximately twice a month.

      If you are interested in joining us for a game, please email uchicagopsipress@gmail.com with your name, affiliation to any existing members, and a demonstration of your sense of humor. Applications are open year-round, with rolling admissions. Typical admission processes include some behavioral screening, as well as some deductive puzzle-solving.

      How the ratings work

      1. Team strength and win chance

        Teams come from the Win and Loss lists. A team’s rating is its players’ average Base Elo going into the game.

        P(Good wins) = 1 / (1 + 10^((EvilAvg + EvilAdvantage − GoodAvg) / 400))

        Equal teams: 50%. A 400-point gap: about 91%. Evil advantage: 0.

      2. Zero-sum swings, scaled by team size

        Good player:  +K × wG × (S − P)      wG = N / (2 × #Good)
Evil player:  −K × wE × (S − P)      wE = N / (2 × #Evil)

        S is 1 if Good won, else 0. K is 100. Smaller teams swing more per player, and gains and losses always cancel.

      3. The cap

        f = MIN(1, K / MAX(|rawGood|, |rawEvil|))      final change = raw × f

        Example: 10 Good vs 2 Evil, equal teams, Evil wins. Raw change is Evil +150, Good −30, so f = 0.667: Evil +100, Good −20.

      4. Uncertainty

        RD = 1 / √(1/RD0² + games × ln(10)² / 400² / 4)      range = Elo ± 1.96 × RD

        RD0 is 150. Shown for players under 5 games. It assumes even matchups, so read it as a minimum.

      5. Chess scale and titles

        Chess Elo = MAX(100, 1500 + stretch × (Base Elo − 1000))

        Stretch is 3.96, which puts the top player near 2850. Display only.

      6. Aura

        Aura = MAX(Base Elo − 1000, 0) × games played

        Rewards staying above 1000 over many games.

      7. Tier lists

        S at or above the first cutoff, then A to D, otherwise F.

      Travellers and Storytellers aren’t in the Win/Loss lists, so they aren’t rated.
