Lore

Why is there Blood on my Clocktower??

The Ψ Press is an invite-only UChicago-based group that plays Blood on the Clocktower approximately twice a month. We first started playing on January 25th, 2025, and have run twenty-eight games with a rotating cast of thirty-six participants, through both online and in-person formats. If you are interested in joining us for a game, please email uchicagopsipress@gmail.com with your name, affiliation to any existing members, and a demonstration of your sense of humor. Applications are open year-round, with rolling admissions. Typical admission processes include some behavioral screening, as well as some deductive puzzle-solving.
