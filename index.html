Lore

Why is there Blood on my Clocktower??

The Ψ Press is an invite-only UChicago-based group that plays Blood on the Clocktower approximately twice a month.

If you are interested in joining us for a game, please email uchicagopsipress@gmail.com with your name, affiliation to any existing members, and a demonstration of your sense of humor. Applications are open year-round, with rolling admissions. Typical admission processes include some behavioral screening, as well as some deductive puzzle-solving.

Method

How the ratings work

  1. Team strength and win chance

    Each game is Good vs Evil, read from the Win and Loss lists. A team’s rating is the average Base Elo of its players going into the game.

    P(Good wins) = 1 / (1 + 10^((EvilAvg + EvilAdvantage − GoodAvg) / 400))

    Equal teams give 50%. A 400-point gap gives about 91%. The Evil advantage is 0.

  2. Zero-sum swings, scaled by team size

    Good player:  +K × wG × (S − P)      wG = N / (2 × #Good)
Evil player:  −K × wE × (S − P)      wE = N / (2 × #Evil)

    S is 1 if Good won, 0 if Evil won; K is 100. A lone Evil player carries more of the swing than one of ten Good players, and total gains and losses cancel exactly, so the sum of all ratings never changes.

  3. The cap

    f = MIN(1, K / MAX(|rawGood|, |rawEvil|))      final change = raw × f

    Example: 12 players, 10 Good and 2 Evil, equal teams, Evil wins. Raw change is Evil +150, Good −30. 150 exceeds 100, so f = 0.667 and the final change is Evil +100, Good −20. Still zero-sum: 2 × 100 = 10 × 20.

  4. Uncertainty

    RD = 1 / √(1/RD0² + games × ln(10)² / 400² / 4)      range = Elo ± 1.96 × RD

    RD0 is 150. Players under 5 games show this 95% range. Treat it as a minimum: it assumes every game was a 50/50 matchup.

  5. Chess scale and titles

    Chess Elo = MAX(100, 1500 + stretch × (Base Elo − 1000))

    The stretch is 3.96, tuned so the #1 player lands near 2850. Only the display changes; the swing cap stays in Base Elo points.

  6. Aura

    Aura = MAX(Base Elo − 1000, 0) × games played

    Aura rewards being good over a long stretch. It is just as valid as Elo: Elo measures the peak of your powers, Aura measures how long you have been wielding them.

  7. Tier lists

    Tiers compare a number to fixed cutoffs: S at or above the first, then A, B, C, D, otherwise F. Rookies are tiered by Elo percentile (share of rated players with a lower Elo), upsets by upset win rate, duos by win rate together.

Travellers and Storytellers are not in the Win/Loss lists, so they are not rated for that game. Everything on this site recalculates from the Games Sheet the moment a game is added.
